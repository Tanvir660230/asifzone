# `tahafi` preset

- `hang-tag.tsx`: the garment hang-tag on a brass thread (`--tag-thread`), its shape from the `.ui-hang-tag` recipe in globals.css. Its text is split at `|` into name, detail and price.
- `hero.tsx`: the classic `Hero` with the small line moved onto the hang-tag over the photograph. Without a photograph it falls back to the classic hero unchanged.

Everything else renders the classic components, styled by the `tahafi` tokens. Brand rules: docs/brand/tahafi/THEME_BLUEPRINT.md.
